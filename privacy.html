Privacy

Last updated November 7, 2026

This page covers projectboki.com. The game itself, itch.io and Discord have their own policies.

What we collect

Visits. We use PostHog to count visits and see which pages and buttons people use, such as the Play and Discord buttons. This includes your browser, device type, rough location from your IP address, and the page that sent you here.

Your email, if you give it. If you sign up for playtest invites, we store your email address so we can contact you about the game.

We don't ask for anything else, and we never sell or share your data with advertisers.

Cookies

Until you choose, analytics runs without cookies or storing anything on your device. If you click Accept, PostHog stores an ID in a cookie and your browser's storage so return visits are linked. If you click Decline, nothing is stored except your choice. You can change it any time with the Cookies link at the bottom of the page.

Who handles it

Analytics and signups are stored with PostHog on servers in the United States. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, or to stop playtest emails, email orri@projectboki.com. We keep signup emails until you ask us to remove them or we stop running playtests.

Changes

If this page changes, we'll update the date at the top.
