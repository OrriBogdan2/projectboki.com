About these heights

Ours: the exact .glb files the game loads on main, posed in the held idle frame, at the size a match draws them (Models.hunter scale × Hunter.VIS_SCALE ), with weapons and props, no team tint.

Reference: true height in metres, in the standing idle pose, all turned to face the camera, no scaling (the reference mesh is drawn at 1.00 in its game).

Body height = the crown of the head, feet on the floor: no hats, ears, horns, weapons or props. Tallest point = the highest point of the whole model, shown as a second number. Pairs are ordered by the reference's size on screen in a match (biggest first), which is what a player sees; crown height alone can order two hunters differently.

Our world body_scale is 1.16 (our base hunter 2.04 m vs the reference 1.76 m, decision 0061).
