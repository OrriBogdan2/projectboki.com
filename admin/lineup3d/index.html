About these heights

Ours: the exact .glb files the game loads on main, posed in the held idle frame, at the size a match draws them (Models.hunter scale × Hunter.VIS_SCALE ), with weapons and props, no team tint.

Reference: true height in metres, in their rest pose (those files have no idle clip; idle heights are in each hunter's details).

Head = top of the head without loose props (our head-top anchor; the reference crown). With weapon = the highest point of the whole model.

Our world body_scale is 1.16 (our base hunter 2.04 m vs the reference 1.76 m, decision 0061).
