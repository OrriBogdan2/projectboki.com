In one line

A skill-based PvPvE extraction game for PC where you raise infinitely unique creatures and bring them on every run.

Description

20 to 40 players drop into each match in solo, duo or trio squads, with fast top-down combat. Every player wears a Mask, the relic of a fallen Hunter, and brings their Boki. At higher tiers, go down and another squad can take your Boki home; you can track who took it and try to negotiate it back. Extract and it comes back with you, to be raised: each Boki has its own personality, size, mutations and evolutions.

The Wyrm broke the world and stole everyone's faces. What's left floats above the clouds as the Skylands, and every Hunter is after the Wyrmheart: one wish to remake the world, or rule it.

Fair play

No loot boxes, paid eggs, energy or timers. Boki and power are never sold. Players pay for the game and cosmetics. Open source under GPL-3.0.
