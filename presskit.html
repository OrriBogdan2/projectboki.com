In one line

Project Boki is a creature-raising game where you catch new creatures in deadly extractions. It plays like a MOBA, with WASD controls and real physics.

Description

20 to 40 players drop into each match in squads of three, with fast top-down combat. Every player carries a Hunter's Relic, the Mask of a fallen Hunter, and brings their Boki. At higher tiers, go down and another squad can take your Boki home; you can track who took it and try to negotiate it back. Extract and it comes back with you, to be raised: each Boki has its own personality, size, mutations and evolutions.

The Solmere Kingdom has broken apart. Only those with a Hunter's soul can piece the fallen Kingdom back together.

Fair play

No loot boxes, paid eggs, energy or timers. Boki and power are never sold. Players pay for the game and cosmetics. Open source under GPL-3.0.
