Description

Project Boki is a creature-raising farm and a PvPvE extraction game in one. Players raise Boki, creatures each built from one or two real animals, on a floating-island farm. Then they take them into 20-player expeditions across the sky islands, where Boki ride on the player's shoulder, get thrown into fights, and fight on their own. On the higher tiers, the Boki you carry can be looted by rival squads.

History

Project Boki started in October 2026 as a browser prototype called Kinfall and was rebuilt in Godot as a desktop game. It was renamed Project Boki at the end of October 2026. It's being built in the open, with the community invited to design Boki of their own.

Features

  • Boki with personalities: bravery, loyalty, temper and curiosity drive how each one fights, and a Boki may refuse an order it thinks is reckless.
  • Throw and recall: Boki ride on your shoulder until you throw them into a fight.
  • Shared bond meter and bond ultimates: an armor form or a giant form.
  • Three expedition tiers, from the safe Meadow to the Hollow, where Boki can be captured.
  • Gliding between floating islands, boss lairs and rare boss eggs.
  • Boki learn to talk as their level and trust grow.
  • Shimmering variants, breeding and an original folk-style soundtrack.
  • Open source; Boki are pure data so anyone can design one.
