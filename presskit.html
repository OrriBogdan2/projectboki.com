In one line

A skill-based PvPvE extraction game for PC where your companion creature comes on every run, and you can lose it.

Description

Project Boki puts 20 to 40 players into each match, in solo, duo or trio squads, with top-down combat and six archetypes: Thief, Archer, Mage, Gunslinger, Juggernaut and Bulwark. Every player brings their Boki. It shapes how they fight, and another squad can take it from them. Extract, and it comes home. At home, players raise their Boki; each one has its own memory, personality, size, markings and history.

The game is set in the Skylands, the floating remains of a world broken by the Wyrm in the Sundering. Boki live there as wildlife, each species with its own habitats and behaviour.

Fair play

  • No paid eggs, rolls or loot boxes. No energy or timers.
  • Boki and power are never sold. Players pay for the game or a subscription, and cosmetics.
  • Open source under GPL-3.0.

History

Project Boki started in October 2026 as a browser prototype called Kinfall and was rebuilt in Godot as a PC game. It was renamed Project Boki at the end of October 2026 and is built in the open with a community of former SUPERVIVE players.
